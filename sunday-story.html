The Last Letter

Published on August 23, 2026

Some letters are written to be read immediately. Others wait patiently for the right moment.

Years had passed since the letter was written, but the words inside it had never changed.

One quiet Sunday morning, it finally reached the person it was always meant for.

And sometimes, a single letter is enough to bring an entire past back to life.
